QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Got queries? We have got answers. If your question is not listed here, reach out to our student helpline.

Who is eligible to participate in Vibrance 2026? +

Any currently enrolled undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma student from any recognized university or college across India is eligible. A valid, physical or government-verified digital college ID card with a photograph is strictly mandatory at the entrance gates.

Are on-the-spot registrations allowed during the festival? +

On-the-spot registrations for select competitive events will be open at the Registration Desk (Zone E) between 8:30 AM and 10:30 AM daily, strictly subject to slot availability. For flagship events like Battle of the Bands, Choreo Night, and Fashion Show, advance online registration is strongly recommended as slots fill up fast!

Is accommodation available for outstation college contingents? +

Yes! We offer separate campus hostel accommodations for male and female outstation participants. It includes clean bedding, 24/7 security surveillance, Wi-Fi, and breakfast at a subsidized cost of ₹350 per person per night. Please check the accommodation box during registration or reach out to our Hospitality Team.

What items are prohibited inside the campus festival grounds? +

In accordance with college safety protocols, alcohol, tobacco, vape devices, sharp objects, fireworks, professional DSLR cameras without prior press accreditation, and outside commercial food packages are strictly prohibited. Security bag checks are conducted at all entry gates.

When and how will cash prizes and certificates be awarded? +

Winners of each competition will be officially announced on the stage immediately following jury deliberation. Trophies and formal dummy cheques will be awarded during the Grand Valedictory Ceremony on Sunday, April 19 at 5:00 PM. Cash prizes are transferred via direct NEFT / UPI to the team leader within 3 bank working days. Verified digital certificates are emailed to all registered participants.
